Salesforce AI Assistant

What Can the AI Chatbot Do?

A complete overview of capabilities for sales reps, managers, and business users. The assistant lives inside Salesforce — no separate tool needed.

Record Intelligence — Ask Questions, Get Instant Answers
📋
Summarize Any Record
Get a full overview of an Account, Contact, Opportunity, Case, or Lead — including key details, open items, and related records — in seconds.
Summarize this record Give me an overview
📞
Meeting Prep Brief
Before a call, get a pre-call brief with key contacts, open deals, recent activity, risks, and suggested talking points — all in one click.
Meeting prep Prep me for this call
📈
Open Opportunities
See all open deals linked to the current account or contact — with stage, amount, close date, and health status.
Open Opportunities What deals are open?
⚠️
Risks & Anomalies
Surface hidden risks: stalled deals, overdue tasks, inactive accounts, unresolved cases, or anything that needs urgent attention.
What are the risks? Any red flags?
✅
Tasks & Next Steps
See all open and overdue tasks linked to a record, and get AI-suggested next steps based on the current situation.
Show overdue tasks What are the next steps?
👥
Key Contacts
See all contacts associated with an account or opportunity, including their roles, titles, and contact details.
Key contacts Who are the stakeholders?
📊
KPIs & Metrics
Ask for numbers, performance metrics, or pipeline values and get a visual KPI card view with trend indicators.
Show pipeline value What's the deal size?
📅
Activity Timeline
An interactive horizontal timeline of all Tasks & Events on an Account — clustered by date with a TODAY marker, engagement-coverage bar, and per-source labels (Salesforce / Gong / SalesLoft). Click any cluster to see the activities and jump to the record. Available on the Account page and at the top of an account summary in chat.
Show activity history What happened recently?
🌐
Latest Company News (from the Web)
When you summarize an Account, the assistant searches the web for recent company news and shows a grounded "Results from the Web" list — each item with a clickable Source link. Useful for walking into a call informed on the latest developments.
Summarize this account What's the latest news?
Record Actions — Create & Update Without Leaving the Chat
Action Supported Objects Example Prompt
Create a record Account, Contact, Task, Case, Opportunity, Quote "Create a follow-up task for Acme Corp due Friday"
Update a record Account, Contact, Task, Case, Opportunity, Quote "Update the stage of this opportunity to Closed Won"
Log a call Task (Type = Call, Status = Completed) "Log a call with John Smith, discussed renewal pricing, 30 minutes" — contact and account linked automatically
Duplicate protection All objects AI warns if a matching record already exists before saving
Auto-linking All objects New records are automatically linked to the current page's account or opportunity

Email & Navigation
✉️
Send AI Responses by Email
Any AI response can be emailed directly from the chat. Enter an email address and hit send — no copy-pasting needed. Useful for sharing summaries or briefings with colleagues.
📝
Draft Follow-up Email
Ask the AI to draft a professional follow-up email based on your call — it uses the contact's name, deal context, and next steps from the record. Review and send in one click.
Draft follow-up email
🔗
Click-Through to Records
Any record name mentioned in an AI response is a clickable link. Click it to navigate directly to that Salesforce record — no searching required.

• The AI only sees data you have access to in Salesforce
• Press Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line
